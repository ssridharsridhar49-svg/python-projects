AUTOCRAFT / OUR APPROACH

Every ride has
a point of view.

AutoCraft gives car and bike enthusiasts a simple way to shape a build, keep its details, and refine the idea over time.

01

Two focused studios

Cars and bikes have separate catalogs, routes, and relevant accessory choices so every build starts with the right platform.

02

Your builds stay saved

Registration uses Django’s account system. Passwords are hashed, and saved designs remain linked to your account in the database.

03

Built to be managed

Site administrators can manage users, vehicles, saved designs, and contact messages through Django Admin.
